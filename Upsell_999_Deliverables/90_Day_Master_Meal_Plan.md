# The 90-Day Master Diabetic Meal Plan

Welcome to your 90-Day Transformation. The goal of this plan is not to give you 90 different days of complex cooking. The goal is **Consistency, Simplicity, and Control**.

We use a "4-Week Rotating Matrix". You will cycle through these 4 weeks three times. Research shows that eating similar meals makes tracking easier and stabilizes blood sugar faster than eating something completely new every day.

## Rules for the Next 90 Days
1. **Never skip breakfast.** Eat within 60 minutes of waking up.
2. **Follow the sequence:** Always eat your salad/veggies first, protein second, and roti/rice last.
3. **Drink water:** 1 glass of water 15 minutes before every meal.

## WEEK 1 & 5 & 9 (The Reset Weeks)
*Focus: Very low GI, high protein.*

**Monday**
- **Breakfast:** 2 Moong Dal Chillas with Mint Chutney.
- **Lunch:** 1 Katori Masoor Dal, 1 Katori Bhindi Sabzi, 1 Multigrain Roti, Cucumber Salad.
- **Dinner:** 1 Large bowl of Clear Chicken/Vegetable Soup + Paneer Bhurji.

**Tuesday**
- **Breakfast:** 1 Bowl of Savory Oats (Upma style) with peanuts and peas.
- **Lunch:** 1 Katori Rajma (kidney beans), 1/2 Katori Brown Rice, 1 Katori Curd.
- **Dinner:** Grilled Chicken Tikka OR Soya Chaap (dry) + Green Salad.

## WEEK 2 & 6 & 10 (The Balance Weeks)
*Focus: Introducing complex carbs safely.*

**Monday**
- **Breakfast:** 2 Egg Omelette with lots of spinach (or Besan Chilla for veg).
- **Lunch:** 1 Katori Chana Dal, 1 Katori Lauki Sabzi, 1 Bajra Roti.
- **Dinner:** 1 Bowl of Dal Khichdi (made with equal parts dal and brown rice) + Ghee.

**Tuesday**
- **Breakfast:** Poha (with peanuts and extra veggies, very little flattened rice).
- **Lunch:** Palak Paneer, 1 Roti, Onion Salad.
- **Dinner:** Mixed Veg Stir-fry with Tofu or Paneer.

---

## WEEK 3 & 7 & 11 (The Sustenance Weeks)
*Focus: Everyday Indian home cooking, modified.*

**Monday**
- **Breakfast:** Sprouted Moong Salad with lemon and black pepper.
- **Lunch:** 1 Katori Toor Dal, 1 Katori Cabbage Sabzi, 1 Jowar Roti.
- **Dinner:** Baingan Bharta + 1 Roti + Raita.

**Tuesday**
- **Breakfast:** 1 Bowl of Broken Wheat (Dalia) porridge (savory).
- **Lunch:** Fish Curry OR Soya Wadi Curry, 1/2 Katori Brown Rice.
- **Dinner:** Bottle Gourd (Lauki) Soup + 2 Boiled Eggs (or roasted Chana).

---

## WEEK 4 & 8 & 12 (The Maintenance Weeks)
*Focus: Lifestyle integration.*

**Monday**
- **Breakfast:** 2 slices of Whole Wheat Bread with Peanut Butter (unsweetened).
- **Lunch:** Mushroom Matar Sabzi, 1 Katori Dal, 1 Roti.
- **Dinner:** Chicken Stew OR Mix Veg Stew (coconut milk based) + 1 Roti.

**Tuesday**
- **Breakfast:** 2 Idlis with Sambar (lots of veggies in Sambar).
- **Lunch:** Black Chana Curry, 1 Katori Curd, 1 Roti.
- **Dinner:** Salad bowl with roasted Paneer/Chicken and olive oil dressing.

# Welcome to Your 90-Day Transformation

The goal of this plan is not to give you 90 different days of complex cooking. The goal is **Consistency, Simplicity, and Total Sugar Control**. 

We use a <span class="highlight">4-Week Rotating Matrix</span>. You will cycle through these 4 weeks three times. Research shows that eating similar meals makes tracking easier, reduces grocery waste, and stabilizes blood sugar faster than eating something completely new every day.

## The 3 Golden Rules
1. **Never skip breakfast.** Eat within 60 minutes of waking up to prevent liver-dump sugar spikes.
2. **Follow the sequence:** Always eat your salad/veggies first, protein second, and roti/rice last.
3. **Drink water:** Drink 1 glass of water 15 minutes before every major meal.

<div class="alert">
<strong>Pro Tip:</strong> You can swap any Lunch and Dinner on the same day if you prefer. All meals are calibrated for a balanced glycemic load.
</div>

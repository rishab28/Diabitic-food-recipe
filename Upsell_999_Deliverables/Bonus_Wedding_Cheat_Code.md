# Bonus #1: The Indian Wedding & Festival "Cheat Code"

## How to Enjoy Shaadis Without Spiking Your Blood Sugar to 300

Weddings and festivals are the hardest times for anyone managing diabetes. But you don't have to sit in the corner with a plain salad while everyone else enjoys the feast. 

Follow this "Cheat Code" protocol to keep your sugar stable:

### 1. The Pre-Wedding Anchor
Never arrive at a wedding starving. 30 minutes before you leave home, eat a high-protein, high-fat snack. 
*Example:* A handful of almonds + 1 boiled egg OR a small bowl of paneer tikka. This creates a "buffer" in your stomach that slows down the absorption of carbs later.

### 2. The Buffet Sequence (Crucial!)
When you hit the buffet, the order in which you eat matters just as much as what you eat. Always follow this sequence:
**Step 1:** Eat Veggies/Salad first (Fibre coats the stomach).
**Step 2:** Eat Proteins and Fats second (Paneer, Chicken, Dal).
**Step 3:** Eat Carbs last (Roti, Rice, Naan). 

### 3. The "One Sweet" Rule
You can have sweets! But follow the rules:
- Never eat a sweet on an empty stomach. Always eat it immediately *after* your protein-heavy dinner.
- Pick milk/nut-based sweets (Rasmalai, Kaju Katli) over deep-fried/syrup-soaked sweets (Jalebi, Gulab Jamun). The fat in milk/nuts slows down sugar absorption.

### 4. The 10-Minute Walk
After eating, do not sit down immediately. Walk around, meet people, and stay on your feet for at least 10-15 minutes. This uses up the glucose in your bloodstream immediately.

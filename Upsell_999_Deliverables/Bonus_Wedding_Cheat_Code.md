# The Buffet Survival Protocol

Indian weddings and festivals are notoriously difficult for diabetics. The buffets are filled with refined carbs, deep-fried snacks, and heavy sweets. 

But you do not have to sit in the corner eating a plain salad while everyone else celebrates. You just need a **Strategy**. 

## Phase 1: The Pre-Game (Before you leave home)
The biggest mistake you can make is arriving at a wedding hungry. When you are starving, your brain will crave high-GI carbs (like naan and sweets) immediately.
- **The 30-Minute Hack:** 30 minutes before you leave your house, eat a handful of almonds OR a small bowl of Greek Yogurt (Dahi). This introduces fat and protein into your stomach, which will slow down the absorption of any carbs you eat later.
- **Hydration:** Drink 2 large glasses of water before you enter the venue.

## Phase 2: The Starters Strategy
When the waiters come around with snacks, follow the "Protein First" rule.

## Phase 3: Navigating the Main Course Buffet
The order in which you put food on your plate (and in your mouth) determines your blood sugar spike.

### The "Plate Mapping" Technique:
1. **Fill 50% of your plate FIRST with Salad and Veggies.** Green salad, cucumbers, mixed veg sabzi (without heavy gravy). Eat this *first*. The fiber creates a mesh in your stomach.
2. **Fill 25% with Protein.** Dal Makhani, Chicken Curry, Mutton Curry, or Palak Paneer. Eat this *second*.
3. **Fill 25% with Carbs.** Choose Roti over Naan (Naan is maida/refined flour). Choose a small portion of Rice over Biryani (Biryani often has hidden fats and excessive carbs). Eat this *last*.

## Phase 4: The Dessert Dilemma
You can have dessert. But you must be smart about it.
- **Rule of Half:** Take whatever dessert you want, and immediately cut it in half. Leave the other half on the buffet table. 
- **The Better Choices:** Rasgulla (squeeze out the sugar syrup first!) is better than Gulab Jamun (deep-fried). Gajar Ka Halwa (in moderation) is better than Jalebi (pure deep-fried sugar).
- **The Timing:** Eat the dessert *immediately* after your main course. Do not wait 2 hours. Eating it right after a high-fiber/high-protein meal blunts the sugar spike.

## Post-Wedding Action
Do not go straight to bed. When you get home, take a 15-minute brisk walk. This simple act uses up the glucose circulating in your blood before your body has to release massive amounts of insulin.

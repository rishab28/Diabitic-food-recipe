# Bonus #2: The "Safe Snacking" Guide

## What to Eat at 5 PM With Your Chai So You Don't Feel Deprived

The 5 PM to 7 PM window is when most diets fail. You are tired from work, you have your evening chai, and you crave something crunchy and salty (like namkeen or biscuits). 

Those highly processed snacks will spike your blood sugar immediately. Here is your "Safe Snacking" protocol.

### The Golden Rule of Snacking
A safe snack must contain either **Protein** or **Fibre**. If it's just plain carbs (like a Marie biscuit), it's a trap.

### Top 5 Safe Indian Snacks for Diabetics

1. **Roasted Makhana (Fox Nuts)**
   - *Why it works:* Low glycemic index, high in protein and fibre.
   - *How to eat:* Roast them in 1 spoon of ghee with black pepper and rock salt. Keep a large jar on your desk.

2. **Sprouted Moong Chaat**
   - *Why it works:* Sprouting increases the protein content and makes it incredibly easy to digest.
   - *How to eat:* Add chopped cucumber, tomatoes, green chillies, and a squeeze of lemon.

3. **Roasted Chana (Chickpeas)**
   - *Why it works:* Excellent source of dietary fibre. Keeps you full for hours.
   - *How to eat:* Eat a handful with your evening tea instead of biscuits.

4. **Paneer Tikka Bites (Pan-fried)**
   - *Why it works:* Pure protein and fat. Zero impact on blood sugar.
   - *How to eat:* Keep marinated paneer cubes in the fridge. Toss 4-5 cubes in a hot pan for 2 minutes when hunger strikes.

5. **Mixed Seeds & Nuts**
   - *Why it works:* Healthy fats stabilize blood sugar levels and kill cravings.
   - *How to eat:* A mix of almonds, walnuts, pumpkin seeds, and flaxseeds. Just one small handful is enough.

### What About Biscuits?
Even "Digestive" or "Sugar-Free" biscuits contain refined flour (Maida) and maltodextrin. Avoid them. Stick to whole foods!

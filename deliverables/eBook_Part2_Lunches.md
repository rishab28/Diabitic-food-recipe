# CHAPTER 2: Lunches & Dinners (The Satiety Meals)
*The secret to a stable post-meal reading is the "Plate Method": Half your plate must be low-starch vegetables, a quarter should be protein, and only the final quarter should be complex carbs.*

### 26. Karela (Bitter Gourd) & Onion Sabzi
*GI Rating: Extremely Low | Prep: 15m | Cook: 15m*
**Why it works:** Karela contains charantin, a compound proven to lower blood glucose.
*   **Ingredients:** 2 Karelas (sliced thin, rubbed with salt, squeezed), 2 Onions (sliced), ½ tsp Haldi, 1 tsp Coriander powder, ½ tsp Amchur (Dry Mango Powder), 1 tbsp Mustard Oil.
*   **Method:** Heat mustard oil to smoking point, cool slightly. Sauté onions until golden. Add the squeezed karela. Cook on low heat until crisp. Add spices at the end.
*   **Pair with:** 1 Jowar Roti and a bowl of fresh curd.

### 27. Cauliflower Rice Vegetable Biryani
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 15m*
**Why it works:** Grated cauliflower feels and looks exactly like rice, but has 80% fewer carbohydrates.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 medium Cauliflower (grated into rice-sized grains), Mixed Veggies (Beans, Peas, Carrots), Whole Biryani spices (Tej Patta, Laung, Elaichi), 1 tsp Ghee.
*   **Method:** Temper whole spices in ghee. Sauté mixed veggies until cooked. Add the grated cauliflower. Cover and steam for just 3-4 minutes (do not overcook or it becomes mushy). 

### 28. Chicken Palak (Spinach Chicken)
*GI Rating: Zero/Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 25m*
**Why it works:** Pure protein from chicken mixed with the iron and fiber of spinach. A perfect zero-spike meal.
*   **Ingredients:** 250g Chicken breast pieces, 1 large bunch Palak (blanched and pureed), Onion-Tomato masala base, Garlic.
*   **Method:** Sauté chicken in the masala base until sealed. Add palak puree. Simmer for 15 minutes. 
*   **Pair with:** Cauliflower rice or 1 Multigrain Roti.

### 29. Bhindi Masala (Mustard Oil Okra)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 15m*
**Why it works:** The sticky mucilage in okra binds to sugar in the intestines, slowing its absorption into the blood.
*   **Method:** Slit bhindi. Sauté in mustard oil with cumin, coriander powder, and a generous squeeze of lemon (acid further lowers GI).

### 30. Fish Tikka Masala (Dairy-Free)
*GI Rating: Zero | Prep: 20m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Fish provides Omega-3 fatty acids which reduce insulin resistance.
*   **Method:** Marinate fish pieces in ginger-garlic paste and spices. Pan-fry lightly. Toss in a tomato-onion gravy (no cashew paste or cream).

### 31. Soya Chunks Matar (Peas) Curry
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 15m*
**Why it works:** Soya is the highest protein vegetarian source.
*   **Method:** Soak and squeeze soya chunks. Cook with green peas in a standard tomato gravy. The high protein blunts the carb impact of the peas.

### 32. Eggplant (Baingan) Bharta
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 15m | Cook: 15m*
**Why it works:** Eggplant is incredibly low in calories and carbs but high in fiber.
*   **Method:** Fire-roast the eggplant, mash it. Sauté with lots of garlic, onions, tomatoes, and green chillies.

### 33. Dry Rajma (Kidney Bean) Sundal
*GI Rating: Low-Medium | Prep: 10m (pre-boiled) | Cook: 5m*
**Why it works:** Instead of a heavy gravy, eat Rajma dry-tossed with coconut.
*   **Method:** Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves. Add boiled Rajma. Toss with grated coconut and lemon juice.

### 34. Mixed Dal Tadka (Panchratna Dal)
*GI Rating: Medium | Prep: 10m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Mixing 5 different dals provides a complete amino acid profile.
*   **Method:** Boil equal parts Toor, Moong, Masoor, Urad, and Chana dal. Temper with garlic, jeera, and pure ghee.

### 35. Bottle Gourd (Lauki) Kofta (Non-Fried)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 15m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Instead of deep-frying, we steam or air-fry the koftas.
*   **Method:** Grate lauki, mix with besan and spices. Steam into balls. Drop into a light tomato gravy.

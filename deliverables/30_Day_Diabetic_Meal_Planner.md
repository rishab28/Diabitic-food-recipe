# 30-Day Diabetic Meal Planner: The Stable Sugar Protocol

Welcome to your 30-Day Meal Planner! The goal of this planner isn't starvation or eating tasteless food. The goal is to retrain your body's insulin response by strategically combining Indian ingredients that digest slowly, keeping your blood sugar completely flat throughout the day.

## How to use this planner:
1. **The Order Matters:** Always try to eat your vegetables (fiber) first, then your protein/fats, and finally your carbohydrates. This simple hack alone can reduce glucose spikes by up to 30%.
2. **Hydration:** Drink a glass of warm water with 1 tsp of Apple Cider Vinegar or Lemon 15 minutes before your lunch and dinner.
3. **Flexibility:** If you don't like a specific meal, just swap it with another meal from the same category (e.g., swap a breakfast for another breakfast).

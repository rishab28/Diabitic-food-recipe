# CHAPTER 4: Rotis, Dals & Chutneys (The Foundation)
*You don't have to give up rotis to control your sugar. You just have to upgrade the flour you use. Here are the ultimate low-GI bread replacements and the high-protein dals to pair them with.*

### 76. The "Diabetic Master Atta" (Multigrain Blend)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 5m | Cook: 0m*
**Why it works:** Plain whole wheat (Gehun) spikes blood sugar almost as fast as white sugar for many diabetics. By cutting it with fiber and protein-rich flours, we drastically lower the GI.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 kg Whole Wheat Flour, 500g Besan (Chickpea Flour), 250g Jowar Flour.
*   **Method:** Mix these thoroughly in a large bin. Use this exact blend for ALL your daily rotis. Do not sieve the flour; the bran (chhilka) is pure fiber.

### 77. The "Zero-Spike" Almond & Coconut Roti
*GI Rating: Very Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 10m*
**Why it works:** Almonds and coconut are virtually carb-free. This is perfect for days when your sugar is running unusually high.
*   **Ingredients:** ½ cup Almond flour, ½ cup Desiccated Coconut powder, 1 tbsp Psyllium Husk (Isabgol) for binding, warm water.
*   **Method:** Mix dry ingredients. Add warm water slowly to knead a soft dough. Roll between two sheets of parchment paper (it breaks easily otherwise). Cook on a tawa with ghee.

### 78. Bajra & Methi Roti
*GI Rating: Low-Medium | Prep: 10m | Cook: 10m*
**Why it works:** Bajra (Pearl Millet) is complex and takes hours to digest, keeping your sugar stable and your stomach full. Methi adds incredible fiber.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 cup Bajra flour, ½ cup chopped fresh Methi leaves, green chilli, salt, warm water.
*   **Method:** Knead the dough with warm water (bajra needs warm water to bind). Pat it into circles using your hands or a plastic sheet. Cook on a hot tawa with a drop of ghee.

### 79. Jowar (Sorghum) Bhakri
*GI Rating: Low-Medium | Prep: 10m | Cook: 10m*
**Why it works:** Jowar is a gluten-free ancient grain that doesn't cause the inflammatory spikes associated with modern wheat.
*   **Ingredients:** Jowar flour, salt, hot water.
*   **Method:** Knead with hot water. Roll out and cook.

### 80. Garlic & Flaxseed Roti
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 5m | Cook: 10m*
**Why it works:** Flaxseeds (Alsi) are rich in Omega-3s and fiber, further lowering the GI of the wheat.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 cup Master Atta (Recipe #76), 2 tbsp ground Flaxseeds, 1 tsp Garlic paste.
*   **Method:** Knead into a dough, roll, and cook.

## The High-Protein Dals
*Dals contain both carbs and protein. To make them truly diabetic-friendly, we must ensure they are thick, tempered in healthy fat, and paired with low-GI rotis, never white rice.*

### 81. Green Moong Dal Tadka (Whole)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: Overnight | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Whole moong (with the green skin) has double the fiber of yellow, split moong dal.
*   **Method:** Pressure cook soaked whole green moong. Temper heavily with garlic, jeera, hing, and ghee.

### 82. Chana Dal with Lauki
*GI Rating: Very Low | Prep: 20m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Chana dal has the lowest GI (GI: 8) of all Indian lentils. Adding lauki (bottle gourd) bulks it up with zero-calorie water and fiber.
*   **Method:** Boil chana dal and cubed lauki together. Temper with mustard seeds, curry leaves, and dried red chillies.

### 83. Rajma (Kidney Beans) Kashmiri Style
*GI Rating: Low-Medium | Prep: Overnight | Cook: 30m*
**Why it works:** Rajma has a low GI, but portion control is key. Eat 1 katori of Rajma with lots of salad, not an entire plate of rice.
*   **Method:** Boil rajma. Make a gravy with ginger powder (saunth) and fennel powder (saunf), skipping the heavy onion-tomato paste.

### 84. Masoor Dal (Whole Brown Lentils)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 20m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** High in iron and fiber.
*   **Method:** Pressure cook whole brown masoor. Add a simple onion-tomato tadka.

### 85. Spinach & Urad Dal (Dal Saag)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 30m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Combining heavy urad dal with spinach lowers the overall glycemic load and adds massive volume.
*   **Method:** Boil black urad dal. In a separate pan, sauté chopped spinach. Mix the two and temper with garlic and ghee.

## Sugar-Free Chutneys
*Commercial chutneys and ketchups are packed with liquid sugar. These homemade versions use natural acids and herbs to create intense flavor without the spike.*

### 86. Mint-Coriander Chutney (The Ultimate Green Chutney)
*GI Rating: Zero | Prep: 5m*
**Why it works:** Pure herbs, zero carbs.
*   **Method:** Blend fresh mint, coriander, green chillies, garlic, a piece of ginger, and lemon juice. Add a tiny amount of water. (DO NOT ADD SUGAR OR GUD).

### 87. Peanut & Coconut Chutney
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 10m*
**Why it works:** High fat, high protein. Perfect for pairing with Dosa or Upma.
*   **Method:** Blend roasted peanuts, fresh coconut, green chillies, and tamarind paste. Temper with mustard seeds and curry leaves.

### 88. Garlic & Red Chilli Chutney (Lehsan Ki Chutney)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 10m*
**Why it works:** Garlic acts as a natural blood thinner and improves insulin sensitivity.
*   **Method:** Grind 15 cloves of garlic with soaked dry red chillies, cumin, and salt. Cook in 1 tbsp of mustard oil until the raw smell leaves.

### 89. Roasted Tomato & Onion Chutney
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 15m*
**Why it works:** A perfect replacement for sugary tomato ketchup.
*   **Method:** Roast tomatoes and onions on an open flame. Peel and mash them. Mix with chopped green chillies, coriander, and mustard oil.

### 90. Flaxseed (Alsi) Dry Chutney Powder
*GI Rating: Zero | Prep: 10m*
**Why it works:** Sprinkle this on any meal to instantly add fiber and Omega-3s.
*   **Method:** Dry roast flaxseeds, dry red chillies, garlic, and curry leaves. Grind into a coarse powder.

## 📱 SECTION 1: HOW TO USE THIS TRACKER

### Setting Up in Google Sheets (Recommended — Free & Auto-Calculates)

**Step 1:** Open Google Drive (drive.google.com). Click "+ New" → "Google Sheets."

**Step 2:** Name your file: `My Sugar & Medicine Tracker — [Your Name] — [Year]`

**Step 3:** Create the following tabs at the bottom of the sheet by clicking the "+" icon:
- Tab 1: `Daily Sugar Log`
- Tab 2: `Medicine Log`
- Tab 3: `Monthly Summary`
- Tab 4: `Food Response Log`
- Tab 5: `Exercise & Lifestyle`

**Step 4:** Copy each table from this document into the corresponding tab.

**Step 5:** Apply Conditional Formatting (for automatic color coding):
- Select the data range in the Daily Sugar Log
- Go to Format → Conditional Formatting
- Add rules:
  - `If value < 140` → Fill color: Light Green (#d9ead3)
  - `If value is between 140 and 180` → Fill color: Light Yellow (#fff2cc)
  - `If value > 180` → Fill color: Light Red (#f4cccc)

**Step 6:** Share with your doctor: File → Share → "Copy Link" → Send to doctor on WhatsApp before appointments.

---

### Setting Up in Microsoft Excel (Offline Version)

**Step 1:** Open Microsoft Excel. Create a new workbook.

**Step 2:** Create worksheets using the tabs at the bottom: Right-click "Sheet1" → Rename.

**Step 3:** Apply Conditional Formatting:
- Select data range → Home tab → Conditional Formatting → New Rule
- Use "Format cells that contain" → Cell Value → Less than → 140 → Format → Green fill
- Repeat for 140–180 (Yellow), Greater than 180 (Red)

**Step 4:** For weekly averages, use the formula: `=AVERAGE(B2:B8)` (adjust cell range as needed)

## 🍽️ SECTION 5: FOOD-TO-SUGAR RESPONSE LOG
*This is the most powerful section. It tells you YOUR personal food triggers — not generic advice.*

### How to Use:
1. Before eating, note your blood sugar (pre-meal).
2. Eat ONLY the food you want to test (no mixed meals for accurate testing).
3. After 2 hours, check sugar again.
4. Calculate the spike: Post-meal − Pre-meal = Spike.
5. If spike is under 30 mg/dL = this food is SAFE for you personally.
6. If spike is 30–60 mg/dL = eat this food in smaller portions.
7. If spike is above 60 mg/dL = AVOID this food or discuss with your dietitian.

## 🩺 SECTION 7: TIPS FOR READING YOUR NUMBERS

### What to Tell Your Doctor (Script)
When you go to your next appointment, hand your doctor this printed log and say:
> *"Doctor, I have been tracking my blood sugar, food responses, and exercise for the past 30 days using this structured log. Could you review my trends and tell me if my current medication needs adjustment based on this data?"*

This approach will impress any doctor and lead to a much more productive consultation.

### Warning Signs — When to Call Your Doctor IMMEDIATELY
- Fasting sugar consistently above **250 mg/dL** for 3+ days
- Post-meal sugar above **300 mg/dL**
- Symptoms of hypoglycemia (shaking, sweating, confusion) with reading below **70 mg/dL**
- HbA1c increase of more than **0.5%** in one month

### Positive Milestones to Celebrate 🎉
- Fasting sugar under **110 mg/dL** consistently — Excellent!
- Post-meal under **140 mg/dL** — Outstanding!
- HbA1c drop of **0.5%** — Celebrate this with your family!
- HbA1c under **7.0%** — Your diabetes is well-controlled!

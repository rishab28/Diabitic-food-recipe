# The Indian Diabetic GI Cheat Sheet

The Glycemic Index (GI) measures how quickly a food spikes your blood sugar. 
*   **Low GI (0-55):** Safe, slow digesting.
*   **Medium GI (56-69):** Eat in moderation.
*   **High GI (70-100):** Danger zone, rapid sugar spikes.

Use this color-coded guide for common Indian foods to make split-second healthy decisions.

---

## 🟢 GREEN ZONE (Eat Freely)
*Low GI (0-55). These foods digest slowly, keep you full, and do not cause blood sugar spikes.*

### Vegetables
*   **Karela (Bitter Gourd):** GI 15 *(Excellent for blood sugar control)*
*   **Bhindi (Okra):** GI 20
*   **Palak (Spinach) & Leafy Greens:** GI <15
*   **Lauki (Bottle Gourd):** GI 15
*   **Phool Gobi (Cauliflower):** GI 15
*   **Patta Gobi (Cabbage):** GI 10
*   **Baingan (Eggplant):** GI 15

### Grains & Pulses
*   **Chana Dal:** GI 8 *(One of the lowest!)*
*   **Rajma (Kidney Beans):** GI 24
*   **Moong Dal (Whole):** GI 29
*   **Kala Chana (Black Chickpeas):** GI 33
*   **Jowar (Sorghum):** GI 49
*   **Bajra (Pearl Millet):** GI 54
*   **Steel Cut Oats:** GI 53

### Dairy & Proteins
*   **Eggs, Chicken, Fish:** GI 0 *(No carbs, no spike)*
*   **Paneer (Cottage Cheese):** GI 27
*   **Curd/Dahi (Unsweetened):** GI 28
*   **Milk (Full Cream):** GI 31

### Snacks & Fruits
*   **Roasted Makhana:** GI 55
*   **Peanuts (Roasted):** GI 14
*   **Jamun (Black Plum):** GI 25 *(Highly recommended for diabetics)*
*   **Amrood (Guava):** GI 29
*   **Apple:** GI 38

---

## 🟡 YELLOW ZONE (Eat in Moderation / Combine with Protein)
*Medium GI (56-69). You can eat these, but keep the portion size small (half a katori) and ALWAYS pair them with a Green Zone protein or vegetable.*

### Grains & Pulses
*   **Brown Rice:** GI 68
*   **Whole Wheat Roti (100% Atta):** GI 62 *(Better if mixed with Besan)*
*   **Ragi (Finger Millet):** GI 65 *(High in calcium, but medium GI, so don't overeat)*
*   **Sweet Corn:** GI 60

### Vegetables
*   **Sweet Potato (Shakarkandi):** GI 61 *(Boiled)*
*   **Beetroot:** GI 64

### Fruits
*   **Papaya:** GI 60
*   **Banana (Ripe):** GI 62
*   **Mango:** GI 56-60 *(Eat only 1 slice, strictly post-meal, not empty stomach)*

---

## 🔴 RED ZONE (Avoid or Strictly Limit)
*High GI (70-100). These act like pure sugar in your bloodstream, causing immediate and dangerous spikes.*

### Grains & Flours
*   **White Rice (Polished):** GI 73 - 89
*   **Maida (Refined Flour) / Naan / Kulcha:** GI 71+
*   **White Bread:** GI 75
*   **Instant Oats:** GI 83 *(Avoid these! Use steel-cut instead)*
*   **Poha (Flattened Rice):** GI 70+ *(Unless heavily mixed with peanuts and veggies)*

### Vegetables
*   **Potato (Aloo) Boiled/Mashed:** GI 82 - 90
*   **Pumpkin (Kaddu):** GI 75

### Fruits
*   **Watermelon (Tarbuj):** GI 76 *(High GI, though low glycemic load)*
*   **Dates (Khajoor):** GI 103 *(Extreme spike!)*

### Sweeteners & Snacks
*   **White Sugar / Glucose:** GI 100
*   **Jaggery (Gud) / Honey:** GI 80 - 87 *(Do NOT fall for the myth that Gud is safe for diabetics!)*
*   **Biscuits (Marie / Digestive):** GI 70+
*   **Fruit Juices (Even fresh without sugar):** GI 70+ *(The fiber is stripped out, leaving pure fructose)*

---

### 💡 Pro-Tip to Lower the GI of Any Meal:
Add 1 teaspoon of **Desi Ghee** or eat a small bowl of **Cucumber/Tomato Salad** *before* you eat your carbs. The fat and fiber act like a "net," slowing down the absorption of sugar into your blood!

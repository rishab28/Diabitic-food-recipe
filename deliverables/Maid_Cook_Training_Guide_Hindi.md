# Cook / Maid Training Sheet (Hinglish)
## Ghar Ke Cook / Maid Ke Liye Sugar-Safe Khana Banane Ke Niyam

Agar aapke ghar me khana banane ke liye koi Cook ya Maid aate hain, toh yeh guide unke liye hai. Ise print karke apni kitchen me chipka dein ya unke saath WhatsApp par share karein.

---

## 🛑 Rule 1: Tel Aur Ghee Ka Maap (Oil & Ghee Control)
*Subzi ya dal me andaze se tel dalna band karein.*

*   **Niyam:** Har subzi ya dal ke tadke ke liye sirf **1 chota chammach (1 Teaspoon) tel ya ghee** ka use karein.
*   **Method:** Kadhai me seedhe bottle se tel na dalein. Chammach se naapkar hi dalein.
*   **Kaunsa Tel Use Karna Hai:** Sirf **Sarso ka tel (Mustard Oil)** ya **Shuddh Cow Ghee**. Refined sunflower, soyabean oil bilkul use nahi karna hai.

---

## 🍚 Rule 2: Chawal Ka Smart Swap (Smart Rice Cooking)
*Normal safed chawal plate me nahi parosna hai.*

*   **Niyam:** Agar chawal banana hai, toh sirf **Brown Rice** ya **Low-GI Diabex Rice** hi banayein.
*   **Pakaney Ka Tarika:** Chawal ko pressure cooker me na pakayein. Pateeli me khule paani me ubalein aur pakaney ke baad uska **Mand (sada paani/Starch) poori tarah se chhaan kar alag phenk dein**.
*   **Gobi Chawal:** Agar Cauliflower Rice bola gaya hai, toh gobi ko kaddookas (grate) karke tave par 2 min halke ghee me roast karein.

---

## 🫓 Rule 3: Roti Banane Ka Sahi Tarika (Perfect Roti Blend)
*Sirf gehu ke aate ki saadi roti nahi banegi.*

*   **Aate Ka Ratio (Blend):** 
    *   **50% (Aadha):** Normal gehu ka aata ya Sugar Control Atta.
    *   **50% (Aadha):** Ragi ka aata ya Jowar ka aata.
    *   **Mix Karne Ka Niyam:** Dono ko sukhe aate me achhe se milayein, phir gungune paani se goondhein taaki roti naram bane.
*   **Ghee Ka Niyam:** Tave se roti utarne ke baad us par sirf **1/4 chammach desi ghee** lagayein. Zada ghee ya butter na lagayein.

---

## 🍯 Rule 4: Meethe Par Paabandi (Zero Sugar Rule)
*Chai, doodh ya kisi bhi khane me chini/gud ka use bilkul mana hai.*

*   **Niyam:** Safed chini (Sugar), Gud (Jaggery), ya Honey (Shahad) ka use bilkul nahi karna hai.
*   **Sugar Substitute:** Meethe ke liye sirf kitchen me rakhi **Stevia Drops** ya **Monk Fruit Powder** ka hi use karein.
*   **Chai Ka Niyam:** Chai me doodh kam dalein, paani zada rakhein, aur chini ki jagah last me 2 drops Stevia dalein.

---

## 🥦 Rule 5: Subzi Aur Salad Ki Quantity (More Fiber, Less Carbs)
*Thali me subzi zada aur roti/rice kam hona chahiye.*

*   **Niyam 1:** Aloo (Potato), Arbi aur Shakarkand (Sweet Potato) ka use subziyon me na karein.
*   **Niyam 2:** Har meal ke saath **Salad (kheera, tamatar, pyaaz) bareek kaat kar** pehle parosein.
*   **Niyam 3:** Dal ko gaadha banayein (paani jaisi patli na ho) aur usme paalak ya methi daal kar fiber badhayein.

---

> 💡 **Cook/Maid ke liye yaad rakhne wali baat:**
> "Humara maqsad hai khane ka taste na badle, bas healthy cheezein use karein. Roti patli ho, subzi hari ho, aur tel-masala kam se कम (minimum) ho."

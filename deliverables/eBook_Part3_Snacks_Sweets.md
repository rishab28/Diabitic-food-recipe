# CHAPTER 3: Snacks & Sweets (The Craving Killers)
*When the 4 PM slump hits, reaching for a biscuit or namkeen is the worst thing you can do for your blood sugar. Use these high-protein, healthy-fat snacks to crush cravings.*

### 51. Roasted Garlic Makhana (Fox Nuts)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 2m | Cook: 10m*
**Why it works:** Makhana is low in calories, high in protein, and has a very low glycemic index.
*   **Ingredients:** 2 cups Makhana, 1 tbsp Ghee, 1 tsp Garlic powder, Salt, Black pepper.
*   **Method:** Heat ghee in a heavy bottom pan. Add makhana and roast on low flame for 8-10 minutes until crunchy. Add spices at the very end to prevent burning.

### 52. Kala Chana Chaat (Black Chickpea Salad)
*GI Rating: Very Low | Prep: 5m | Cook: 0m (if pre-boiled)*
**Why it works:** Black chickpeas have a tough outer coating of insoluble fiber, meaning they digest incredibly slowly.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 cup boiled Kala Chana, chopped onions, tomatoes, cucumber, green chilli, lemon juice, chaat masala.
*   **Method:** Mix everything in a bowl. Excellent for post-workout or evening hunger.

### 53. Cucumber & Hung Curd Sandwiches (No Bread)
*GI Rating: Very Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 0m*
**Why it works:** Eliminates the bread entirely while keeping the satisfying "sandwich" experience.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 large Cucumber (sliced thick lengthwise), Hung curd, finely chopped veggies (capsicum, carrot), oregano, salt.
*   **Method:** Mix veggies into the hung curd to make a spread. Sandwich the spread between two thick cucumber slices. 

### 54. Roasted Peanuts & Paneer Mix
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 5m | Cook: 5m*
**Why it works:** Pure fat and protein. The ultimate blood-sugar stabilizer.
*   **Ingredients:** ½ cup unsalted roasted peanuts, 50g Paneer (cut into tiny cubes), Chaat masala.
*   **Method:** Lightly toss the paneer cubes on a dry tawa until warm. Mix with peanuts and sprinkle chaat masala.

### 55. Almond Flour "Nankhatai" (Sugar-Free Cookies)
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 15m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** By replacing maida with almond flour and sugar with stevia/erythritol, you get a cookie that won't spike your sugar.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 cup Almond Flour, ¼ cup Besan, ¼ cup Ghee, Stevia powder (to taste), Cardamom powder, chopped pistachios.
*   **Method:** Mix all ingredients into a soft dough. Roll into small balls, flatten slightly. Bake at 160°C for 15-20 minutes. Let them cool completely to crisp up.

### 56. Stevia-Sweetened Gajar Ka Halwa
*GI Rating: Medium | Prep: 15m | Cook: 30m*
**Why it works:** Carrots have a high GI but a low Glycemic Load. Cooking them in ghee and milk blunts the spike. We remove the sugar entirely.
*   **Ingredients:** 500g Carrots (grated), 2 cups Double-toned Milk, 2 tbsp Ghee, Stevia/Erythritol, Cardamom, Almonds.
*   **Method:** Sauté carrots in ghee. Add milk and simmer until milk reduces completely. Add sweetener and nuts. 

### 57. Chia Seed "Falooda" 
*GI Rating: Zero/Low | Prep: 5m | Cook: 0m*
**Why it works:** Chia seeds are 40% fiber by weight.
*   **Ingredients:** 2 tbsp Chia seeds, 1 cup unsweetened Almond milk or coconut milk, a few drops of Rose essence, Stevia.
*   **Method:** Soak chia seeds in the milk for 30 mins until it forms a gel. Add rose essence and sweetener. Serve chilled.

### 58. Peanut Butter Apple Slices
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 2m | Cook: 0m*
**Why it works:** The fat in the peanut butter slows down the absorption of the fructose in the apple.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 Apple (sliced), 2 tbsp Unsweetened Peanut Butter (check the label, no added sugar!).
*   **Method:** Dip apple slices in peanut butter. 

### 59. Roasted Pumpkin & Sunflower Seeds
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 5m | Cook: 5m*
**Why it works:** Magnesium-rich seeds improve insulin sensitivity.
*   **Method:** Dry roast seeds on a tawa with a pinch of salt. Store in an airtight jar.

### 60. Lauki (Bottle Gourd) Kheer
*GI Rating: Low | Prep: 10m | Cook: 20m*
**Why it works:** Replaces the high-carb rice in traditional kheer with zero-carb lauki.
*   **Ingredients:** 1 cup grated Lauki (squeezed dry), 2 cups Milk, Ghee, Stevia, Cardamom.
*   **Method:** Sauté lauki in ghee. Boil in milk until thick. Add sweetener and nuts.

## 📋 HOW THIS PLAN WORKS

### The Science Behind It (In Simple Language)
Every food you eat gets broken down into sugar (glucose) in your blood. The **Glycemic Index (GI)** is a score from 0–100 that tells you how fast a food raises your blood sugar.

| GI Score | Category | What it means |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 0–55 | 🟢 Low GI | Releases sugar slowly. SAFE & IDEAL for you. |
| 56–69 | 🟡 Medium GI | Moderate sugar release. Eat in small portions. |
| 70–100 | 🔴 High GI | Rapid sugar spike. Avoid or minimize. |

### 3 Golden Rules for This Week
1. **Never skip meals.** Skipping meals causes a dangerous sugar crash followed by a spike.
2. **Eat within 30 minutes of waking up.** This stabilizes the morning cortisol-sugar spike.
3. **Walk for 10–15 minutes after lunch and dinner.** This alone can reduce post-meal sugar by 20–30 mg/dL.

### What to Expect This Week
- **Day 1–2:** Your body is adjusting. You may feel slightly less full than usual (normal).
- **Day 3–4:** Energy starts stabilizing. Post-meal sluggishness reduces.
- **Day 5–7:** Noticeable reduction in post-meal sugar spikes. Better sleep quality.

*Theme: Introduce your body to low-GI eating without shocking it.*

### ☀️ Breakfast (7:30 AM – 8:30 AM)
**Moong Dal Chilla (Green Moong Pancakes) with Green Chutney**

| Detail | Value |
|:---|:---|
| **Quantity** | 2 medium chillas + 2 tbsp green chutney |
| **GI Score** | 38 (Low GI ✅) |
| **Prep Time** | 15 minutes |
| **Calories** | ~220 kcal |
| **Protein** | 12g |

**Why it works:** Moong dal is one of India's best diabetic foods. High in plant protein and fiber, it slows glucose absorption dramatically. The green chutney (coriander + mint) contains compounds that actively help insulin sensitivity.

**Quick Recipe:**
- Soak 1 cup whole moong dal overnight. Grind to a smooth batter.
- Add salt, jeera, green chilli, grated ginger.
- Make thin pancakes on a non-stick tawa with ½ tsp oil each.
- Serve with fresh coriander-mint chutney.

## 🌅 DAY 3 — THE FIBER FOCUS
*Theme: Flood your system with fiber to slow every glucose spike.*

### ☀️ Breakfast (7:30 AM – 8:30 AM)
**Oats Idli (4–5 pieces) with Sambar and Coconut Chutney**

| Detail | Value |
|:---|:---|
| **Quantity** | 4–5 oats idlis + 1 katori sambar + 2 tbsp chutney |
| **GI Score** | Oats Idli: 44 (Low ✅) |
| **Prep Time** | 20 mins (batter prep the night before) |
| **Calories** | ~280 kcal |

**Quick Recipe:**
- Dry roast 1 cup oats. Cool and powder coarsely.
- Mix with ½ cup curd, salt, eno (½ tsp). Rest 10 mins.
- Steam in idli moulds for 10–12 minutes.
- Serve hot with fresh sambar (use lots of vegetables — drumstick, tomato, onion).

**Why it works:** Oats contain beta-glucan — a special fiber that forms a thick gel in the stomach, dramatically slowing glucose absorption. Regular oat consumption reduces HbA1c over 3–6 months.

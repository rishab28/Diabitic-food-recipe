## 4. The FAQ Responses (Save these in WhatsApp Business Quick Replies)

**If they ask: "Can I eat potatoes?"**
"Hi! Potatoes (Aloo) have a very high Glycemic Index and will spike your sugar quickly. I highly recommend swapping them with Sweet Potatoes (Shakarkandi) in moderation, or better yet, using raw bananas (Kaccha Kela) in curries which behave very similarly but have a lower GI!"

**If they ask: "Can I use Jaggery (Gud) instead of Sugar?"**
"This is a common myth! Unfortunately, Gud and Honey spike blood sugar almost exactly the same way white sugar does. For your teas and sweets, I highly recommend using Stevia drops or Erythritol. They are safe, natural, and have zero impact on blood sugar."

**If they ask: "I don't like X ingredient, what can I use?"**
"No problem! You can easily swap that out. As a general rule, you can swap any green vegetable with another green vegetable (e.g., Palak for Methi), or any protein with another protein (e.g., Paneer for Tofu or Soya). Let me know the specific recipe and I'll give you a replacement!"

## 💡 Support Boundary Rules for You
1. **Never give medical advice.** Always state: *"I can help with the recipes, but please consult your doctor regarding your insulin dosage or medication."*
2. **Set a specific time.** Only reply to messages between 4 PM and 5 PM. If you reply instantly all day, they will treat you like a 24/7 chatbot.
3. **Use Voice Notes:** A 15-second voice note saying "Hi Priya, yes you can use Jowar instead of Bajra!" builds massive trust and takes less time than typing.

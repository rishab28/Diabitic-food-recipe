The 5-Minute "Tiffin Trick"

Humare system ke peeche ka simple 3-step formula jo picky eaters ke upar kaam karta hai:

1. SWAP (Badlav)

2. SHAPE (Roop)

Bachche sabse pehle aankhon se khate hain. Normal paratha ko Star paratha ya Pinwheel rolls banao. Unhe lagega ye koi fancy market food hai!

3. SNEAK (Chhipana)

Palak ko roti ke dough mein mix karo, beetroots ko pink sauce pasta mein chhipao. Bachche ko pata bhi nahi chalega aur wo saari sabziyaan khush ho kar khayega.
